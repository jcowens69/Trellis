The best time to call is before they think to call you.

We structure records so the moments that matter surface on their own, wherever that data lives. It requires the data to be structured well enough that the moment doesn't get buried in an old spreadsheet.

Beyond the transaction

Your past clients are your best lead list – if you can still find them.

The transaction ends, but the relationship is where the referrals come from. When a closed deal becomes a dead record instead of a maintained relationship, the agent loses the repeat business, the referral, and the review – without ever knowing they were on the table.

01

Repeat-client history

Every past transaction linked to the client, so the fact that you sold them their first house shows up when they're ready for their second.

02

Referral source tracking

Which past client, agent, or lender sent you your last five deals – tracked like the pipeline it is, not remembered from memory.

03

Household & family connections

A client's adult kids, siblings, and parents connected as one network, since real estate referrals travel through family faster than almost any other channel.

04

Sphere-of-influence contacts

Past clients, vendors, and community contacts who aren't "leads" in the traditional sense, but are exactly who your business actually runs on.
