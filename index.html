The problem

Nobody notices the tangle until the migration is already underway.

Duplicate contacts get counted twice. Lapsed fields quietly corrupt reports. It's what happens when cleanup keeps losing to the closer deadline – pushed to "once we're live" until the spreadsheet meant to hold things over outlives the system it was standing in for. By the time an implementation partner opens the export, there's no time left to plan around what they find.

What we do

Root it in an audit. Grow from there.

01

Data Health Audit

A closer look at what's actually going on in your database – how much is duplicated, which fields nobody trusts anymore, where the real migration risk is hiding. You walk away with real numbers, not guesses.

02

Data Migration Prep

The unglamorous work: names, phone numbers, addresses, and dates made consistent across every record, categories and tags cleaned up, transactions aligned and formatted correctly. Duplicates get merged instead of migrated twice.

03

Migration Management

You choose how involved we get. We'll help you pick the new platform, translate your team's needs into a real spec, then run point with the software implementer – from a sounding board to full ownership. Seed to bloom, and everywhere in between. See how it works →

04

Post-Migration Assistance

The stretch after the implementer's contract ends and before the system actually feels right – a focused, one-month engagement to fix the small stuff that only shows up once your team starts using it day to day.

05

Ongoing Data Management

A standing arrangement to keep the database from sliding back into the state you just paid to get it out of – database administration, continued data cleaning and hygiene, and regular reporting and analytics, evolving the system as your needs change instead of letting it quietly decay.
